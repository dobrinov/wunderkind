# Practice modes: topics, problem types, review

„Тренирай тема" (`/practice/topics`, `TopicPracticesController`, a row on „Упражнения") is a session of one topic the student picks: ten fresh problems from `Dispatcher.pick` aimed at the student's rating in that topic, at most `SessionComposer::MAX_PER_SHAPE` per template, measured like any practice, and allowed past the grade hold because the topic is named (`TopicPractice`). The list is `PracticeFocuses.offer` (leaves with enough problems near the child), and „Слаби места" — on that page and on „Аз" — is `TopicPractice.weakest`: played topics (`MIN_GAMES`) rated furthest below the student's own rating. Every exam-age child in the family field test asked for exactly this. „Типови задачи" (`/practice/types`, `ProblemTypesController`, a row on „Упражнения") groups problems by the idea a child needs rather than by topic — „Възрастови задачи", „Везни и равновесие", „Работа назад от края" — because Bulgarian teachers and competition books think in these and a child preparing for a competition wants „ten more like that one". The list is `config/problem_types.yml` (slug, `name` read in the list, `short` under it, `description` on the type's page, a real `example`), written by a person and read by `ProblemTypes`; a question carries one slug in `questions.problem_type`, set from its problem file's `type:` — a past paper's problem by its own, ours written on its model inheriting the paper task's (`PastPapers.modelled_from`). A type is listed once `MIN_PUBLISHED` published problems carry it; its page shows the idea, the example, and where it was set (the real past-paper problems of it, as source labels); a session is ten of it at the child's level through `Dispatcher.pick(problem_type:)`, easiest first — the point is to meet one idea and climb with it — measured like any practice. The first 38 types were written for the Софийски математически турнир, 3rd grade; 25 more („Кой знак липсва“, „Проценти“, „НОД и НОК“, „Нормален вид на многочлен“…) came with grades 1 and 5–7, so the list runs from first grade to algebra and is ordered nearest the child first (`ProblemTypes.offered(user)`: the distance from each type's median Elo to the student's target). One-off problems carry no type. A student can read their own record back at `/review` (`ReviewsController`, „За преговор"), two tabs over one shape: the questions they got wrong and the ones they marked „не съм го учил". `AnswerLog` is the query, and its whole point is that both lists are of *questions*, not of answers — nothing in `Dispatcher` excludes a question the student has already seen, so `user_answers.where(correct: false)` would keep a question got wrong in March and right in April on the list forever, which is the opposite of what the list is for. Only the latest answer to each question counts (`DISTINCT ON`, Postgres, which this app already is), so the list drains as the student fixes things and a question leaves it by being answered correctly — or by being retired, since a question that is never served again could never be answered correctly and would sit on the list for good (a draft one stays: withdrawing from the report queue is reversible), or — a mistake only — by waiting `AnswerLog::MISTAKE_DAYS` (21) without a second try: in the meantime the child has practised and usually moved on, and one production card had grown to „100 задачи чакат втори опит“, two thirds of them over two weeks old. The answer stays in the history. A `pending_review` free-text answer is excluded by name rather than by `correct: false` — it is waiting for a grader, not marked down. The mistakes tab offers `MistakePractice`, a session of `kind: :mistakes` built from those questions oldest-first (a mistake made this morning is still in mind; one made three weeks ago is the only one re-asking tells us anything about) and filtered through `practice_pool` but *not* through the deferral rules, because a wrong answer is not a skip and the student is the one choosing. That session is the one solo session that **measures nothing**: `Assignment#measured?` is false for it, and `AnswerSubmission` runs only its second half — grading, explanations, XP, streak, badges and the session bonus all happen, while the student's rating, the question's Elo, the `skills` row, its spaced-review date and its mastery do not move. The reason is the same one that keeps duels out of the measurements: these are questions whose worked explanation the student has already read, so a correct answer is recall, and recording it as ability would hand out rating for remembering an answer while telling the bank that a question only ever retried by the students who failed it is an easy one. Not moving the ratings is only half of that, which is what `UserAnswer.measured` is for: `attempted` means "not skipped" and is the *effort* scope (the history card, the accuracy, the badges, all of which a mistakes session genuinely earns), while `measured` additionally drops mistakes sessions and is read by the three places that take an answer as *evidence* — `Dispatcher.calibrating?`, the `player_games`/`task_games` K-factor, and `PerformanceTrend`, whose whole chart is "the difficulty of what you can get right" and would otherwise rise on recall. The XP has one carve-out for the same reason: `Xp::ATTEMPT_AMOUNT` buys the risk of attempting, an unmeasured session risks nothing, and a wrong answer there leaves the question on the list it came from — so a wrong answer in a mistakes session pays nothing, while a correct one takes the question off the list and pays by when the mistake was made (`Xp.for_mistake_redone`): half the same day, when it is recall of a worked answer just read (a child in the family field test earned 164 XP in 96 seconds that way), in full in between, and `RECALL_BONUS` times over from `RECALL_AFTER_DAYS` on, which is what spaced practice is for. A mistakes session earns no coin: recall is not learning (Coins). „Не съм го учил" is refused there too (`Assignment#skippable?`, enforced in `AnswersController#skip` and not only hidden in the markup) — the session has already been told, and a skip would move a genuine mistake off the list it was put on to drain. `PracticeHistory` gained a skip tally beside the answer counts: skips stay out of `total_answers`, the accuracy and the minutes (a skip is not practice and the daily goal is a goal for practice) but are counted as `total_skipped` and shown as the fourth stat on the history card, which is also the way through to `/review`.
