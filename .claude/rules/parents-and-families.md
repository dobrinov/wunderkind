# Parents, families, goals, focus, mail

Goals and rewards: a parent can ask something of a child and promise something for it. The parent's **children list** (`/parents/children`, „Семейство" in the menu) is where a parent checks in. It opens on „Днес" for the whole family (`parents/children/_today`): who practised today and how much, who has not and whose streak ends tonight (`ChildOverview#streak_at_risk?`), and how many rewards are owed — with twelve children the cards alone were 8000px on a phone. A family of more than three folds the header's „Кой ще тренира?" into its menu, and the child bar folds the siblings into a menu beyond two. Each child's card carries what a visit is for (`ChildOverview`): when they last practised, this week's days, problems, accuracy and minutes, the live streak (`Streaks.current` — `users.current_streak` is only rewritten on the next practice, so it still shows the number a child stopped at a month ago), the goals with their bars, rewards owed, and two actions: for a managed child „Влез в профила“ is the primary button — a child without an email has no login, so switching in is the only way they ever practise — with „Нова цел“ beside it, while a child with their own login gets „Нова цел“ as the primary (a goal set from the list returns there, `return_to=children`). The detail page is a plain link. `/parents/children/:id` is the observation space (`Parents::ChildrenController#show`), in two tabs (`?tab=progress`, the default, and `?tab=goals`; owed rewards sit above both, being the one thing there a parent must act on, and goal and reward actions return to the goals tab), and its progress tab is the same performance cards the child sees on „Аз", because there is no second way of drawing nine weeks of practice and a parent sitting down with their child should be looking at the same picture (`home/_history` and `_time_spent` took `linked:`/`user:` locals for it; the active-day links and the review link are the student's own and are dropped on somebody else's page). A `Goal` is three columns out of one sentence — *fifteen minutes a day for a week, and you get an hour of Minecraft*: `metric` (minutes / problems / correct, all read off the **effort** scopes, so a mistakes session counts — a parent promising a reward for practice means practice), `mode` (`daily`, a bar to clear each day, or `total`, a running sum) and `period` (`week`/`month`, which come round again and pay again, or `once` with an end date). A goal set midway through a week or month starts with a shorter period, which asks for its share (`Goals.target_for`: a count of days never more than the days it has, a total in proportion) and says so on the card — set on a Thursday, a five-day week used to be lost the moment it appeared. In `daily` mode `target` is a *count of days* and not „every day" on purpose: a goal a child loses for one bad Tuesday is a goal they stop trying for on Wednesday, and a parent who means every day sets seven. **Progress is never stored** — `Goals` asks `user_answers` the question afresh on every read, the way `PracticeHistory` does, and one `Goals::Tally` covers every period in one query. What *is* stored is `goal_awards`, one row per completed period, carrying a **copy** of the reward's wording: a parent who changes „an hour of Minecraft" to „an ice cream" next week has not changed what they owe for last week. Paying out happens on read (`Goals.refresh!`, from the home page and the parent's page) rather than on answer — a session ends on the summary screen and lands on the home page, which is a better place to be told you have earned something than the feedback card of whichever problem tipped the total, and it keeps `AnswerSubmission` out of this entirely; the unique index on (goal_id, period_start) is what makes a second look cost nothing. The new-goal form is that sentence (`parents/goals/new`): each choice is a `<details>` of radio chips (`_slot`, `.goal-sentence`/`.goal-slot`) whose summary shows the checked word through `:has()`, so it works with no script; under it the child's own card is rendered by `home/_goals` through `GoalsController#preview` into a turbo-frame (`goal_form_controller` refetches on change), so the preview cannot word a goal differently from „Днес", and it shows any validation error before „Обещай" is pressed. A goal is archived rather than edited — it is a promise made in particular words, and rewriting one mid-week changes what a child is working towards without telling them — and archiving never takes back what it already paid. The child's half is `home/_goals`, showing the parent's wording verbatim, which is the half that makes the feature work at all: a reward nobody told the child about motivates nobody. `Parents::RewardsController` marks one used, and only ever used — the list is a record of what a child earned and must not quietly lose an entry.

**What a child practises** („Какво упражнява", the middle tab of `/parents/children/:id`, `?tab=practice`) is general practice or a `PracticeFocus`: up to `MAX_TOPICS` leaf topics, a `target` (`step` — each topic's rating 50 points above where it stood when the focus was set, `starting_ratings`, and the default; or `mastery` — the `skills.mastered_at` rule, which for the youngest topics can take weeks, since „Събиране и изваждане" barely reaches 1400) and an optional reward shown to the child verbatim. Above the topics, a preset per exam (НВО 4/7/10, матура: `Competitions.exams`) ticks the child's `MAX_TOPICS` weakest topics of that exam's programme (`PracticeFocuses.exam_preset`, `?preset=`), saved only when the parent presses „Запази". `PracticeFocuses.offer` lists only leaves with `Curriculum::MINIMUM_PER_TOPIC` published problems within `DuelCategories::REACH` of the child's target, plus the running focus's own topics, which are then flagged as thin rather than dropped. Like a goal, a focus is never edited: `PracticeFocuses.set!` archives whatever is current and creates a new one (carrying a topic's starting rating only under the same target), and a partial unique index allows one running focus per child. Progress is never stored: `PracticeFocuses.refresh!` reads the skills rows on the child's home, the parent's page and the family list, and stamps `completed_at`; the home page celebrates once (`celebrated_at`, not while impersonating), and an owed reward joins the goal awards in the parent's owed box until `Parents::FocusRewardsController` marks it given. While a focus runs, `DailyPractice` passes `PracticeFocuses.session_topic_groups` to `SessionComposer.execute(focus:)`, which picks per unfinished topic at the child's rating in that topic through `Dispatcher.pick(..., reach:)`, never widening past REACH, and fills the rest with the ordinary mix; deferrals still apply, and `skipped_count` tells the parent how many focus problems were marked „не съм го учил". The same tab holds the session length: `DailyPractice::DEFAULT_MINUTES` is 10 (a session a child finishes is one they come back to), `MINUTES_OPTIONS` are 5/10/15/20/30, `Parents::DailyMinutesController` sets it for any linked child, and a child with a linked parent sees the number read-only in Настройки (`ProfilesController` drops the field). Accounts that existed before the default dropped from 15 were written down at 15. Analytics event: `focus_set`.

Roles & social: **teachers, classrooms and homework were removed** (the `teacher` role, `Teachers::` namespace, classroom invites, `HomeworkCreator`, the `:homework` assignment kind and `AnswerOverridesController` are all gone; the drop migration converted teacher users to parents and their homework sessions to plain practice, so student history survived). Parents link to children via `parent_links` (`users.link_code`, shown on the student profile; `Parents::` namespace), and a child too young for an email gets a **managed account**: `User.create_managed_child!` makes a student with no email (`users.email` is nullable, presence required only `unless: :managed?`), a random unused password and `users.managed_by_id` pointing at the parent, plus the usual `parent_links` row. Such an account has no login of its own — it is reached by switching profiles (see Controllers). Creating a child with an email *and* password still works and is also managed, so the parent can switch in; a child linked by code is not managed and cannot be switched into, and an emailless child cannot be unmanaged (nobody would be able to reach them). **Several parents share a family as equals**: *who* may step into a managed child is every parent linked to it through `parent_links` (`User#switchable_children`, the one answer for `acting_child`, `ChildSessionsController`, the header switcher, the child bar and the changelog stamp), not only `managed_by`, which now only says whose account the child's lives inside. Either parent sets and archives goals and focuses, and the owed list is per child, so a „Дадено" from either clears it for both; where both parents see a promise it is credited („обеща Деян", „цел от Мама"), and the child reads it by `users.name_for_children` („Мама иска:", falling back to the first name; set on the family page or when accepting). A second parent comes in by a `FamilyInvitation` from the „Родители" card on `/parents/children`: by email (mailed only while `Mailing.enabled?`, otherwise the card shows the link and code to pass on) or by a six-character code („Нов код" withdraws the old one), seven days, single-use, revocable by any parent of the family. `/family/invitations/:token` is public — sign up as a parent there, or „Влез и приеми" through the sign-in form (`?invitation=`) — and the code goes into the family page's box or the parent sign-up form; accepting (`FamilyInvitation#accept!`) links the invitee to every child the inviter has at that moment, refuses a student account, and is not behind the email gate (the verified inviter chose to share; an invitation mailed to the address that signs up confirms it). Deleting a parent hands each managed child, and the goals and focuses they set for children who still have a parent, to the longest-linked remaining parent; only a child left with no parent is deleted (`User#managed_children_lost_on_destroy` names them). `DailyPractice` sizes a `kind: :daily` session from `daily_minutes_target` and the student's median answer time — placement answers left out, floored at `MIN_SECONDS_PER_QUESTION`, capped at `MAX_QUESTIONS` (12: every child in the family field test quit between problem 7 and 12 of a 30) — and „Още една" resumes today's unfinished daily session (`DailyPractice.unfinished_today`) instead of starting another. `Dispatcher.available_pool` leaves out every question the student answered in the last `Dispatcher::RECENT_DAYS` (14), so yesterday's problems do not come back word for word; the composer's last-resort filler may still use them rather than fail. „Готово за днес" on „Днес" means the minutes were reached or a daily session was finished today, not that something was answered. `Placement.finish!` lifts every unplayed `skills` row (created at the floor while the placement chose its questions) to the placed rating, and topic mastery is only ever awarded on a correct answer. A session left untouched for `StaleSessions::STALE_AFTER` (a day since its last answer — or `UNTOUCHED_AFTER`, an hour, when nothing in it was answered) is closed on the next visit to the home page, „Упражнения" or a day's session list (and `UnfinishedSessions` never offers one that would be), and „Подготви се" resumes the round under way for that event (`CompetitionPractice.unfinished`) rather than starting another — run on read like `Goals.refresh!`, since there is no job runner, and never while impersonating: its unanswered rows go, its answers stay, `completed_at` becomes the time of its last answer and `abandoned_at` marks it, so `Assignment.finished` (read by the session badges) does not count walking away as finishing; a session with no answers is deleted, and placements are left to `Placement`. `Leaderboards.weekly_xp` is a pure query over `xp_events`, feeding the global weekly board. The `private_library`/`in_review` statuses remain — a suggestion waits in review, and returning one parks it in the library. Mail goes through `UserMailer` and `ParentMailer`, and **`Mailing.enabled?` is the switch: it is on exactly when the environment names an SMTP server** (`SMTP_ADDRESS`, with `SMTP_USERNAME`/`SMTP_PASSWORD`/`SMTP_PORT` and an optional `MAIL_FROM`, in `/opt/apps/wunderkind/.env` — `config/environments/production.rb` reads them, since this deploy has no master key). It reads the environment rather than a constant so a deploy without credentials changes nothing: every flow whose only step is "we email you a link" stays switched off rather than failing quietly. One-off mails go `deliver_later` on the default in-process async adapter (there is no job runner). Email verification (`users.verified_at`, `generates_token_for`) gates what an address nobody has proved could do to *somebody else's* child (`Parents::BaseController#require_verified_email`): a parent whose children are all managed accounts acts freely before confirming — adding the first child used to wait on it, and every parent who left without a child had confirmed and then not come back — while inviting a parent (`require_confirmed_address`) still waits, and a child's link code typed before confirming is checked and kept (`users.pending_link_code`, with a fresh confirmation mail) and linked by `User#verify!` — the confirmation link then opens that child — rather than refused, which lost the parents who typed one. The confirmation link's *first* use signs the person in when the browser it opens in has nobody signed in (a phone's mail app is another browser) and takes a parent with no child to the new-child form; the token lives two days, so later uses only confirm. With the switch off nothing is sent on sign-up and `User#verified?` answers true whatever the column holds, so the gate, the `shared/_verification_notice` banner and its resend button go quiet together. **The day the switch first turns on, run `rails mailing:grandfather` once**: every account that existed while mail was off was used without ever confirming its address, and the column starting to count would lock those parents out of their children's goals for want of an email they were never sent — they have been signing in with that address, which is what a confirmation proves. Password reset (`PasswordResetsController`) is closed by `require_mail_delivery` while mail is off (the sign-in page hides „Забравена парола?"); on, it mails a 30-minute link, and completing a reset also stamps `verified_at`, since the link only reached them through that inbox. Changing a password from inside the app (`PasswordsController#update`, `PATCH /profile/password`, in Настройки) works either way: it takes the current password, the new one and a confirmation, then rotates the session id — all three checks in the controller because `has_secure_password` treats an empty `password=` as a no-op, and it refuses while a child's profile is open. `User::MINIMUM_PASSWORD_LENGTH` is named so the form can say the number before the validation does.

**The weekly report** (`WeeklyReport`, `ParentMailer#weekly_report`) is a Sunday-evening email to each parent on the week ending that day (Monday to Sunday, read at 18:00 — later that evening is in no report), one per parent with every child in it, sent by `rake reports:weekly` from cron on the host (`WeeklyReport.week_start_for`: on a Sunday that week, on any other day the last full one, so a missed run can be made up). It was a Monday-morning email until 2026-11-01; Sunday is when a family can still plan the week, which is what its one idea per child is for. Top to bottom: every reward still owed in the family (goal awards and finished focuses, the one thing to act on), then per child the minutes by day as table-cell bars (mail clients drop SVG), the four numbers with the change in problems, a label only for a week beyond any earlier one (`best_week_before`, a grouped count over all the child's answers) or under half a last week of at least `QUIETER_FROM`, the sentences (`harder_weeks` off a nine-week `PerformanceTrend`), the week's coins as „Похвалете за", and at most one suggestion (`suggestion_for`: a small goal for a slipping week with no goal running, else the weakest topic by `TopicPractice.weakest` when no focus is set — never nagging about what a parent already has in hand); a child who did not practise gets one line, and only beside a sibling who did (`quiet_children`). The subject names up to three children by first name and says „the children" beyond that. It is **only sent when there is something in it**: a child who answered nothing that week is left out, and a parent none of whose children practised gets no email at all — a report that says „nothing" every week is one people learn to delete unread. Duels alone do not count, as they do not in the practice history. Every number comes off the services the parent's pages read (`PracticeHistory`, `PerformanceTrend` for whether the solved problems got harder, `Streaks`, `goal_awards` earned that week, new `badge_awards`, `AnswerLog`'s review list), and grown-up framing is allowed (the band and its grades). Recipients are everyone with a child linked to them — a parent, or an admin who is one too — with `users.weekly_report` (default on, switched in Настройки or from the email — see below) and a **confirmed** address — a report about a child must not go to an address typed at sign-up and never shown to be theirs. `users.weekly_report_sent_on` holds the Monday of the last week decided, quiet or sent, so a second run sends nothing twice. **The child reminder** (`ChildReminder`, `ParentMailer#child_reminder`, `rake reminders:children`, daily cron) is two emails at most (`users.child_reminders_sent`, the last at `child_reminder_sent_at`) to a parent account with no `parent_links` row: one at `AFTER` (3 days), and a last one, saying so and pointing to Discord, at `SECOND_AFTER` (8 days) and at least `GAP` after the first, never to an account older than `WINDOW` (14 days) — so the first run did not reach back to every abandoned sign-up. For an unconfirmed address its one button is a fresh confirmation link (the sign-up one expires in two days), which signs the parent in and opens the new-child form. The new-child form is one card — name, birth year, school start — with the child's own email and password and linking by code folded away (`.form-disclosure`), and a failed save renders it again with what was typed. **Every email is Bulgarian**: `ApplicationMailer` wraps each action in `I18n.with_locale(:bg)`, whatever the request, the job or `users.locale` says. **Which emails a person receives** is `EmailPreferences`: the list of optional emails, each a boolean column of the same name on users (default on) and an `audience` saying who it is for — `weekly_report`, for parents and anyone with a linked child, and `report_activity`, for students and the same (`UserMailer#report_activity`, sent from `QuestionReportMessage`'s `after_create_commit` for every message the team writes on a student's report, to the student and every linked parent with a confirmed address — `QuestionReport#people_to_email`; a parent is told it was their child who reported, which is how a managed child's family hears at all — and `UserMailer#report_filed`, to the parents alone, when `QuestionReport.file!` creates a report or reopens a closed one, never when a child rewrites a report still in play); a new optional email is one entry there, its column and its copy under `email_preferences.kinds`. The account emails (confirming an address, a new password) are deliberately not on it: they are sent only because the person just asked, and switching them off would only lock somebody out. The switches appear in two places that read the same list (`shared/_email_switches`): the „Имейли“ card in Настройки (while mail is on, for an account with an address — `ProfilesController` permits only the kinds the user is offered), and `/emails/:token` (`EmailPreferencesController`, a non-expiring `generates_token_for :email_preferences`), reached without signing in from every optional email, with a „stop all“ button. Every such email carries an unsubscribe link and RFC 8058 one-click headers pointing at `/emails/:token/unsubscribe/:kind`: the GET only shows a button, because mail scanners open every link, and the POST — which a mail client sends with no CSRF token — turns that one kind off.
