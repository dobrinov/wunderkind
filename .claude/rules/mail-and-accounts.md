# Mail, verification and email preferences

Managed children, co-parents and goals are in `parents-and-families.md`.

Mail goes through `UserMailer` and `ParentMailer`, and **`Mailing.enabled?` is the switch: it is on exactly when the environment names an SMTP server** (`SMTP_ADDRESS`, with `SMTP_USERNAME`/`SMTP_PASSWORD`/`SMTP_PORT` and an optional `MAIL_FROM`, in `/opt/apps/wunderkind/.env` — `config/environments/production.rb` reads them, since this deploy has no master key). It reads the environment rather than a constant so a deploy without credentials changes nothing: every flow whose only step is "we email you a link" stays switched off rather than failing quietly. One-off mails go `deliver_later` on the default in-process async adapter (there is no job runner). Email verification (`users.verified_at`, `generates_token_for`) gates what an address nobody has proved could do to *somebody else's* child (`Parents::BaseController#require_verified_email`): a parent whose children are all managed accounts acts freely before confirming — adding the first child used to wait on it, and every parent who left without a child had confirmed and then not come back — while inviting a parent (`require_confirmed_address`) still waits, and a child's link code typed before confirming is checked and kept (`users.pending_link_code`, with a fresh confirmation mail) and linked by `User#verify!` — the confirmation link then opens that child — rather than refused, which lost the parents who typed one. The confirmation link's *first* use signs the person in when the browser it opens in has nobody signed in (a phone's mail app is another browser) and takes a parent with no child to the new-child form; the token lives two days, so later uses only confirm. With the switch off nothing is sent on sign-up and `User#verified?` answers true whatever the column holds, so the gate, the `shared/_verification_notice` banner and its resend button go quiet together. **The day the switch first turns on, run `rails mailing:grandfather` once**: every account that existed while mail was off was used without ever confirming its address, and the column starting to count would lock those parents out of their children's goals for want of an email they were never sent — they have been signing in with that address, which is what a confirmation proves. Password reset (`PasswordResetsController`) is closed by `require_mail_delivery` while mail is off (the sign-in page hides „Забравена парола?"); on, it mails a 30-minute link, and completing a reset also stamps `verified_at`, since the link only reached them through that inbox. Changing a password from inside the app (`PasswordsController#update`, `PATCH /profile/password`, in Настройки) works either way: it takes the current password, the new one and a confirmation, then rotates the session id — all three checks in the controller because `has_secure_password` treats an empty `password=` as a no-op, and it refuses while a child's profile is open. `User::MINIMUM_PASSWORD_LENGTH` is named so the form can say the number before the validation does.

**The weekly report** (`WeeklyReport`, `ParentMailer#weekly_report`) is a Sunday-evening email to each parent on the week ending that day (Monday to Sunday, read at 18:00 — later that evening is in no report), one per parent with every child in it, sent by `rake reports:weekly` from cron on the host (`WeeklyReport.week_start_for`: on a Sunday that week, on any other day the last full one, so a missed run can be made up). It was a Monday-morning email until 2026-11-01; Sunday is when a family can still plan the week, which is what its one idea per child is for. Top to bottom: every reward still owed in the family (goal awards and finished focuses, the one thing to act on), then per child the minutes by day as table-cell bars (mail clients drop SVG), the four numbers with the change in problems, a label only for a week beyond any earlier one (`best_week_before`, a grouped count over all the child's answers) or under half a last week of at least `QUIETER_FROM`, the sentences (`harder_weeks` off a nine-week `PerformanceTrend`), the week's coins as „Похвалете за", and at most one suggestion (`suggestion_for`: a small goal for a slipping week with no goal running, else the weakest topic by `TopicPractice.weakest` when no focus is set — never nagging about what a parent already has in hand); a child who did not practise gets one line, and only beside a sibling who did (`quiet_children`). The subject names up to three children by first name and says „the children" beyond that. It is **only sent when there is something in it**: a child who answered nothing that week is left out, and a parent none of whose children practised gets no email at all — a report that says „nothing" every week is one people learn to delete unread. Duels alone do not count, as they do not in the practice history. Every number comes off the services the parent's pages read (`PracticeHistory`, `PerformanceTrend` for whether the solved problems got harder, `Streaks`, `goal_awards` earned that week, new `badge_awards`, `AnswerLog`'s review list), and grown-up framing is allowed (the band and its grades). Recipients are everyone with a child linked to them — a parent, or an admin who is one too — with `users.weekly_report` (default on, switched in Настройки or from the email — see below) and a **confirmed** address — a report about a child must not go to an address typed at sign-up and never shown to be theirs. `users.weekly_report_sent_on` holds the Monday of the last week decided, quiet or sent, so a second run sends nothing twice. **The child reminder** (`ChildReminder`, `ParentMailer#child_reminder`, `rake reminders:children`, daily cron) is two emails at most (`users.child_reminders_sent`, the last at `child_reminder_sent_at`) to a parent account with no `parent_links` row: one at `AFTER` (3 days), and a last one, saying so and pointing to Discord, at `SECOND_AFTER` (8 days) and at least `GAP` after the first, never to an account older than `WINDOW` (14 days) — so the first run did not reach back to every abandoned sign-up. For an unconfirmed address its one button is a fresh confirmation link (the sign-up one expires in two days), which signs the parent in and opens the new-child form. **Every email is Bulgarian**: `ApplicationMailer` wraps each action in `I18n.with_locale(:bg)`, whatever the request, the job or `users.locale` says. **Which emails a person receives** is `EmailPreferences`: the list of optional emails, each a boolean column of the same name on users (default on) and an `audience` saying who it is for — `weekly_report`, for parents and anyone with a linked child, and `report_activity`, for students and the same (`UserMailer#report_activity`, sent from `QuestionReportMessage`'s `after_create_commit` for every message the team writes on a student's report, to the student and every linked parent with a confirmed address — `QuestionReport#people_to_email`; a parent is told it was their child who reported, which is how a managed child's family hears at all — and `UserMailer#report_filed`, to the parents alone, when `QuestionReport.file!` creates a report or reopens a closed one, never when a child rewrites a report still in play); a new optional email is one entry there, its column and its copy under `email_preferences.kinds`. The account emails (confirming an address, a new password) are deliberately not on it: they are sent only because the person just asked, and switching them off would only lock somebody out. The switches appear in two places that read the same list (`shared/_email_switches`): the „Имейли“ card in Настройки (while mail is on, for an account with an address — `ProfilesController` permits only the kinds the user is offered), and `/emails/:token` (`EmailPreferencesController`, a non-expiring `generates_token_for :email_preferences`), reached without signing in from every optional email, with a „stop all“ button. Every such email carries an unsubscribe link and RFC 8058 one-click headers pointing at `/emails/:token/unsubscribe/:kind`: the GET only shows a button, because mail scanners open every link, and the POST — which a mail client sends with no CSRF token — turns that one kind off.

Mail somebody is waiting for — password reset, email confirmation, family invitation — is sent in the request through `Mailing.deliver` (`deliver_now`, a failure reported and the page answering as usual), not `deliver_later`: production has no queue backend, so Active Job is a thread in the web process and a mail queued just before a deploy or restart was lost. Report-activity mail stays on `deliver_later`. An address is stored in lower case (`User.normalizes :email`); sign-in and the reset form always looked it up that way, and one stored as typed could never sign in again.
