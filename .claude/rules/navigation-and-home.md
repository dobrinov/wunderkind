# Navigation, feature rollout, „Днес“ and „Аз“

**Navigation is one menu** (`ApplicationHelper#nav_items`), the same on every screen size: a student gets four places in the header (`shared/_main_nav`, from 640px) and on the phone's tab bar (`shared/_tabbar`) in the same words — **„Днес" · „Упражнения" · „Двубой" · „Аз"** — with line icons (`shared/_icon`, Lucide paths drawn inline so they take the text colour) where there used to be emoji, which every platform draws differently. The header stays on one line down to 640px (icons hidden below `lg`) — it used to wrap to two rows on an iPad mini. „Упражнения" (`/practice`, `PracticesController`) is every way to practise as one list of `.list-row`s: the daily session first, „За преговор", competitions entered, and the competition calendar; it used to be a POST that started a session, which left the review list and competitions reachable only from home-page cards. Friends and the weekly leaderboard are tabs of the duel (`challenges/_tabs`: „Стаи" · „Приятели" · „Класация", on `/challenges`, `/friends` and `/leaderboard`), and leagues are a *section* of „Класация" (`leagues/_section`) rather than a second row of pills — `/leagues` redirects there; the „Двубой" item carries a count of friend requests plus open duel invitations (`duel_waiting_count`, a plain count — `ChallengeMatchmaker.invites_for` sweeps, which must not run on every page). The header's amber chip (`shared/_player_stats`) is the streak and the level and links to „Аз"; it and „Аз" are the only places either appears. Everything that is not a place — settings, suggesting a problem, the changelog, signing out — is behind the avatar (`shared/_account_menu`, on every width; on a phone it also carries a non-student's menu items, since only a student has a tab bar). A parent's one place is „Семейство" (`/parents/children`; `/home` redirects a non-student to their own page), and every managed child is one tap away in the header's „Кой ще тренира?" switcher (`shared/_child_switcher`, `switchable_children`; in the account menu on a phone, hidden while a child's profile is open). **An admin is often a parent too**, and for them the app is two places under one header (`shared/_app_header`, rendered by both the `application` and `admin` layouts): a „Семейство | Администрация" switch (`shared/_workspace_switch`, `ApplicationHelper#admin_workspace` / `workspace_switch?` — shown only to an admin with children or on the family side, so the rest of the team is not handed a „Семейство" they have no use for), each side carrying what waits on the other (`TeamInbox.waiting` on „Администрация", rewards owed on „Семейство"). On the admin side the menu is `admin_nav_items` — Табло · Входящи (with the count) · Задачи · Потребители · Теми · Статистика, words only beside the switch, a scrolling row of chips (`.admin-subnav`) below `xl` — and the design system moved behind the avatar. `/overseer` is „Табло" (`Overseer::DashboardController`): what waits on the team — the tickets, and `Attention`'s two lists of what nobody filed a ticket about: students far below the aim or skipping much of what they get, and questions almost nobody gets right in the last week — beside a glance at the admin's own children (`ChildOverview`) with the shortest way to hand the device over and the rewards owed. Which side the logo and signing in open is `users.admin_landing` (last / family / admin), chosen in the account menu (`AdminLandingsController`); „last" reads a `workspace` cookie the two base controllers write (`ApplicationController#remember_workspace`). Switching into a child from an admin page remembers that page (`session[:admin_return_to]`), and the child bar offers the way back to either side — „Администрация" to that very page.
 Sign-up (`UsersController#new`) is two steps on one URL: with no `?role=` it asks „Кой си ти?" as two `.list-row` links, with one it shows the short form for that role (a post with no role is still a student); links rather than a radio group, so it needs no JavaScript and the back button returns to the choice, and the page is `turbo-cache-control: no-cache` because Turbo's cached preview swallowed what was typed. `?competition=` rides through both choices, but only the student form carries it. The account forms — name, nickname, password, sound, feedback, the parent link code — live on `/settings` (`SettingsController`, still posting to `ProfilesController#update` and `PasswordsController`, which redirect back there); `/profile` is „Аз", about the child. Настройки is grouped rows (`.settings-group` / `.settings-row`: Профил, Тренировка, Имейли, Сигурност, За приложението); the switches (`.switch`, a checkbox with `role="switch"`) and the daily-minutes strip save as they are flipped through `settings_switches_controller.js`, a PATCH to `ProfilesController#update` that answers JSON with the values it actually wrote — anything `profile_params` dropped, a validation error or an impersonated session's 403 puts the control back rather than showing „Запазено". Without JavaScript the same forms post with a save button the controller hides. Typed fields keep one save per group; the password folds into a `<details>` row; the daily goal is a student's only.

**Features arrive one at a time** (`FeatureVisibility`): a new student used to meet about ten feature surfaces on day one. Every secondary feature (`FEATURES`: review, badges, duel, friends, leaderboards, competitions, suggest) is *findable* (a quiet row under „Още в Wunderkind" at the foot of „Аз", or in „Упражнения"), *presented* (a one-time „Ново за теб" sheet on „Днес" — `home/_introduction`, a `<dialog>` answered through `IntroductionsController` — and a row where it is used), or *prominent* (its own tab, a home card, counts). Nothing is locked: the states decide only what the app pushes forward, and every trigger reads something already counted — duels are presented after `SESSIONS_FOR_DUEL` finished sessions or `ANSWERS_FOR_DUEL` answers and become the „Двубой" tab after `DUELS_FOR_TAB` duels or a friend; the review card needs `REVIEW_CARD_AT` practisable mistakes; competitions are presented at `COMPETITION_LEVEL` or within `EXAM_SEASON_DAYS` of an exam round. Someone else starting a feature presents it early, and the „Двубой" tab always shows while a friend request or an invitation is waiting (`duel_nav_item`), so nothing addressed to a child sits behind a tab they have not been shown. The duel's sheet asks for a nickname when there is none — the reason the leaderboard used to greet new students with an empty list. `users.introduced_features` records the sheets seen (one per visit, oldest first, never over the release notes); `users.show_all_features` is the parent's „Покажи всичко веднага" (`Parents::FeaturesController`). A just-placed student gets a three-step tour of „Днес" first (`home/_tour`, `tour_controller.js`, steps found by `data-tour` attributes, `users.toured_at` via `PATCH /tour`); the highlighted element dims the page with a spread shadow, lifting the sticky header with it when the target is inside. Accounts created before it shipped (`FeatureVisibility::GRADUAL_SINCE`) keep every feature prominent and get no sheets — taking „Двубой" out of the menu of a child who had it yesterday read as a feature removed — and were also marked introduced and toured. A new parent's first run is three steps: the empty family page explains them and makes adding a child the button; creating or linking a child lands on that child's „Какво упражнява" tab with a „Стъпка 2 от 3" banner and a „Готово — подай на …" switch-in.

Student home (`/home`, „Днес", `HomeController#show`) answers „what do I do now" and nothing else, in one column: the hero is today — the session button, the minutes bar and the streak's week — then the parent's focus (`home/_focus`) and goals, then only what is actually waiting: a friend's duel invitation and mistakes ready to practise (as `.list-row`s, side by side only when both are there) and the countdown to a competition the child entered. A card with nothing to say is not drawn. It used to be ten blocks with eight to eleven competing calls to action plus a rail of level ring, progress card, leaderboard places and next badge; level and streak are now the header chip and „Аз", the progress card is on „Аз", the leaderboards are under „Двубой", the coins are „Аз"'s „Колекция", and the competition calendar is under „Упражнения". The progress chart and the answers by day are on „Аз" (`profiles/show`, under `#progress` / `#stats`; a day's misses link back there), which shows level and streak once each instead of an XP bar beside three tiles saying the same thing. The route was `/calendar` while the page held a month grid; it is `/home` now, and `/calendar` (and `/calendar/:date/assignments`) stay as permanent redirects for bookmarks and home-screen shortcuts. The month grid of `aspect-square` cells is gone (with it the `Calendar` service and the `shared/svg` partials): history is a nine-week contribution strip built by `PracticeHistory`, whose active days still link to that day's sessions, and above it the one chart on the page: `PerformanceTrend` plots the average Elo of the questions the student answered *correctly* each week over the same nine weeks. Deliberately not accuracy — `Dispatcher` holds every session near `TARGET_SUCCESS_RATE`, so percentage correct is engineered flat and would draw a horizontal line under a child who is improving; what moves is the difficulty. The gridlines are `RatingBand` boundaries rather than round numbers, so the axis reads in the same words as the band card and the raw Elo stays in the point's tooltip. A week the student sat out is a gap, not a zero, and the card does not render until two weeks carry a point. `RatingBand` names Elo ranges (Начинаещ/Уверен/Силен/Отличен/Състезател, the 1400 boundary shared with topic mastery) so a raw rating never leads — but **only for grown-ups**: the parent's page, the landing page and the design system, where its card adds the school grades the rating corresponds to (`RatingBand#grade_span`, off `Curriculum`'s windows). A child is never shown a band, their own or anyone else's. The scale runs from 1st grade to the матура, so nearly every primary-school child sits in the first two bands for years, and a strong eight-year-old reading „Уверен“ hears „average“ — the app knows no ages, so the band cannot say „for your age“, and a parent, who knows the grade, can. The child's „Аз" shows `shared/_progress_card` (`StudentProgress`) instead: the gain over about a month off the same `PerformanceTrend` series the chart draws (shown only when positive — a dip gets a kind sentence, not a number) and the rating cut into nameless 50-point steps, so the next one is always a few answers away. The trend chart draws the band lines without their names on the child's page (`labelled: false`), the practice card no longer labels a problem „Ниво Уверен“, the placement result names a starting point rather than a band, and friends, lobbies and the ready room drop bands — a lobby says „близо до твоето ниво“ when a room is within `ApplicationHelper::NEAR_LEVEL`, and nothing otherwise. Its card draws the bar and the five names from the **same** numbers (`RatingBand.segments`, each band's share of the 800–1800 scale): they used to be a continuous gradient under five evenly-spaced flex items, so a rating of 1013 put the marker at 21% while „Уверен" — the band it is actually in — sat near 30%, and the dot landed to the left of its own label. Two axes drawn from different numbers can only agree by accident. **Awards are coins** (`Coins`, `shared/_coin`, the design canvas's „game coins" board): fifteen tracks, each a row of coins minted in better materials as the child goes — Дърво, Бронз, Желязо, Сребро, Злато, Платина, Диамант, rung *n* in material *n* (a one-coin track, Върнах се, is minted in Диамант). They replaced nineteen medals that production showed mostly arriving on a first day by accident (Ранно пиле on a UTC clock, Гигантоубиец on a mis-rated problem) and rewarding the clock, clicks, XP and recall (perfect_session paid out in mistakes sessions). Each track is something a parent would praise — „Научих я!" (a mistake answered right in a later measured session ≥ 3 days on), Изкачване (rating over `users.climb_base_elo`, set by placement), „Тема напред" (topics 100 over `skills.base_rating`, the first measured rating), „Над нивото" (right, no hint, after calibration, ≥ `ABOVE_LEVEL_GAP` over the student at that moment — `user_answers.rating_before`/`question_rating_before` are recorded with every answer for it), „Чиста серия", „Цяла седмица" (weeks with ≥ 4 practice days, never reset; the streak flame stays in the header), Довърших, „Върнах се", Детектив (reports fixed), Автор (suggestions accepted), „Решиха я", „Удържах обещанието" (goal awards and finished focuses), and Двубои/Реванш, which stay out of sight until duels serve each player at their own level. Every ability track reads `UserAnswer.measured`. Nothing is stored but the coins: a count is a query over rows that exist, and `badge_awards` holds one row per rung (`badge_key` is the track, `rung` 1–7, `seen_at` once shown). `Coins.refresh!` mints after every answer (the answer tracks only) and on „Днес", „Аз" and the parent's pages (the rest); a track that depends on somebody else is hidden until possible (`visible_tracks`). A coin is shown once — on the session summary or „Днес" (`coins/_new`), never mid-problem — in a voice by `SchoolGrade` (a big coin and „Нова монета!" up to 4th grade, one quiet line with its reason from 8th, the middle voice otherwise), and stamped seen only by the child, never while impersonating. „Аз" has „Колекция": one row of slots per track in a fixed order, the next slot ringed by its progress, each row opening a `<dialog>` with the ladder and the dates (`coins_controller.js`). Parents get „Похвали днес" on the family page (`ChildOverview#coins_today`) and the week's coins in `WeeklyReport`. The art is one SVG template with eight palettes and fourteen Lucide symbols (`CoinsHelper`), demoed on `/design-system` under „Монети". At launch the old award rows were deleted and `rake coins:backfill` minted every track from existing rows silently. Not built yet: „Чист списък" (needs a little stored state), the clink sound for the youngest, and seasonal coins (BACKLOG). `math_value`/`shared/_frac` (and `app/javascript/lib/frac.js` for the widgets, and `RichContent.render_math` for question bodies) set a bare "3/4" as a stacked fraction so widget maths matches the typeset question body. All of them (with `math_text` and `lib/authoring.js`'s preview) write the same three spans — the wrapper `role="math"` with an `aria-label` of „3/4", the two numbers `aria-hidden`, because read as loose numbers a screen reader said ¾ as „3 4" — on purpose: a question body and the multiple-choice options under it show fractions inches apart, and an option is a plain string with no document to hold a math node, so KaTeX in one and `.frac` in the other would put two different fractions on one card.
