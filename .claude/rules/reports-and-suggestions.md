# Reports, suggestions and the inbox

Students report broken questions: `question_reports` (question × user, `reason` enum — misleading / wrong_answer / missing_answer / typo / image / broken / other — plus an optional note, `status` open/resolved/dismissed and who closed it). The control is `shared/_report_problem`, a folded-away `<details>` under the answer controls on the practice screen (in both states — a wrong accepted answer is usually only discovered *after* answering) and on the review of a past answer; `QuestionReportsController#create` files it through `QuestionReport.file!`. Like a skip it is not an answer, and unlike a skip it is not even a measurement: reporting grades nothing, moves no Elo, XP, streak or `skills` row, and leaves the question answerable — the only consequence is a row in the admin queue, because the fix is always a human editing the question. A unique index on (question, user) means re-reporting rewrites the student's own row and reopens it rather than stacking duplicates, so the student sees what they said instead of a second form. Reports and suggested problems (below) are **one inbox of tickets**, „Входящи“ at `/overseer/inbox` (`Overseer::InboxController` over `TicketInbox`): three states that split every ticket — „Чакат нас“ (`awaiting_team`, the default), „В разговор“ (`awaiting_reply`: the team wrote last) and „Затворени“ — kind chips Всички / Сигнали / Предложения with counts, one row per ticket (kind and reason, the question, who — „твое дете“ when it is the signed-in admin's child — age, whose turn it is), and the chosen ticket open beside the list in a turbo-frame (`inbox_controller.js` moves the highlight). `filter=active`/`all` and `question_id` still work, because the questions list links with them; the old `/overseer/question_reports` list and `/overseer/reviews` redirect here. A report's ticket, in the inbox pane and on its page, shows what the child last answered to the question and how it was graded (`QuestionReport#reporter_answer`, practice or duel), beside the accepted answer. Each ticket also has its own page, `overseer_question_report_path` (`Overseer::QuestionReportsController#show`): previous/next and „3 от 11“ within the inbox view it came from, the question as the reporter saw it (the snapshot) beside how it is now with what changed, the thread, a composer that writes as „Екипът на Wunderkind“ (and says so again when the reporter is the admin's own child), the state as four choices, the people, the other tickets on the same question (a comment or status change can be applied to the active ones too, „Също и за другите…“) and „Скрий задачата“, which drops the question back to `draft`, the one status no practice session or duel draws from. Every ticket action takes a `return_to`, so the inbox pane and review mode post to the same actions. The waiting count is `QuestionReport.waiting_count` (both kinds; `waiting_counts_by_kind` splits it), and the questions list flags reported rows. A report is a **ticket**: `open` until the team writes, `in_progress` while the two sides talk, closed as `resolved` or `dismissed`. The thread is `question_report_messages` — comments and the state changes between them (`kind`: resolved/dismissed/reopened/withdrawn, and the student's `confirmed`/`not_fixed`), so it reads top to bottom. A comment moves a new report to in progress, and comments are the only way the team writes to the student; the dropdown carries no message of its own and sets in progress (starting or reopening), „Поправена“ — which requires the fixed version, pre-filled with the question itself for a fix made in place and changed for a fix that is another row — or „Няма проблем“, whose required explanation is the one text a status change carries (the fields show through CSS `:has()`, the controller enforces them). Withdrawing keeps the reports *in progress*, since the student is still owed the answer to whether the fix worked. The student answers in the thread at `/reports/:id` (`ReportsController`, `QuestionReport#student_respond!`): „Оправено е“ closes their report, „Още не е наред“ or any message on a closed one reopens it, and `awaiting_team` (new, or the student wrote last) is what the queue sorts first and the admin nav counts. The student is told by email (`report_activity`, see Mailing), on „Днес“ (`home/_report_replies`) and by a count on „Аз“ (`unread`: a team message after `seen_at`, which opening the thread stamps, except while impersonating), and can archive a report (`archived_at`) — the next word from the team brings it back. `QuestionReport.file!` keeps a `snapshot` of the body, options, accepted answer and explanation, so the thread shows what a fix changed (`changes_since_report`); a team message can carry a corrected version (`question_report_messages.corrected_question_id`, typed as a number or pasted as any admin link ending in it — always when resolving, optionally on a comment), and `QuestionReport#current_question` is the latest one named, or the reported question; the admin edit page says how many students are waiting, and „Редактирай задачата“ from a ticket returns to it after saving. A team message that names a corrected version draws it inside the message, read-only (`reports/_question_card`: picture, text, the options with the right one marked or the accepted answer, and the explanation; no form and no widget, an interactive question by its solution), so nobody follows a link to see it, and the top of the thread is then the question as reported with what changed folded under it; with no such message, a report closed as fixed or as not a problem shows the card at the top instead, and an open one shows the question without its answer, since the answer is what may be wrong; re-reporting refreshes it and reopens a closed report. The team speaks as „Екипът на Wunderkind“ — a child is never shown which admin wrote. A linked parent takes part too: the child's page grows a „Сигнали“ tab once there is a report, and `Parents::ReportsController` shows the same question section and conversation with a box to write in (`family_comment!`; on a closed report it reopens it) — no verdict and no archiving, which are the child's. Each message records its `side` (reporter / team / family) rather than inferring it from the author, since a parent can also be an admin; `awaiting_team` is „the last word is not the team's“, and `ReportsHelper` words each author for the reader. Reports closed before threads existed got one closing entry, marked read. Duels report too, through `ChallengeReportsController` (`POST /challenges/:id/reports`) into the same rows and the same queue — reporting is not in the class of things a duel withholds (a hint and a skip would both be worth points to whoever used them fastest; a report awards nothing, skips nothing and stops nothing, so it costs the reporter their own seconds and nobody else's, which `reports.clock_note` says out loud). Mid-match the control sits under the answer controls on the problem in front of the player and comes back to it — re-serving does not re-stamp `question_started_at`, so the report buys no thinking time; on the result screen, the one place the whole match is listed with its answers, `challenges/_report` is a single control with a picker over the five problems (already-reported ones marked ⚑) rather than a flag on each row of a scoreboard. `shared/_report_reasons` is the shared body of every report form, so a duel and a practice question ask the same question in the same words.

Anyone signed in — student, parent or admin — can also *suggest* a problem: `/suggestions` (`SuggestionsController`, plus the `Suggestion` form model) is a lighter door than the admin form — one topic, an optional explanation, no widgets, no status or Elo control — but both write problems in the same **authoring language**. It is a plain `<textarea>` (`shared/_plain_editor`, `plain_editor_controller`), no contenteditable: a line is a paragraph and a bare `3/4` is a fraction. 44px symbol keys sit above it (a mouse press is refused on `pointerdown` and a finger on `touchend`, so neither moves the focus off the text box — see `plain_editor_controller#keepFocus`) — the fraction key adds the bar after a digit, and otherwise puts it in and leaves the caret in front of it, stepping over it when pressed again or when `/` is typed; ², ³, √, π, ·, :, °, ≤, ≥ and ≠ go in at the caret without taking the focus — and a live preview („Така ще изглежда") sits under it. `RichContent.parse_authoring(text, latex:)` is the one parser: without `latex` it is `text_to_doc`, so a typed problem and an imported one are the same document; the admin form turns `latex` on for `$…$` (`\$` is a dollar) and a fuller key row. The preview runs `app/javascript/lib/authoring.js`, a port of the same rules (no regex lookbehind, for older iPads), drawing fractions with the same `.frac` spans; any other formula goes through the lazy `math` controller, so KaTeX is fetched only once a `$` appears. The two parsers are held together by `spec/fixtures/rich_content_cases.json`, run through Ruby and, via `node`, through the JavaScript. A stored question reopens through `RichContent.authoring_text` (fractions as `a/b`, other math as `$…$`), and every shipped problem round-trips to the same document and `body_text`. Bold and italic are not in the language — production had none. `Question` checks its body against the schema (`RichContent.valid?`) whenever it changes. This replaced Tiptap + a MathLive popover: 1.66 MB of JavaScript on the page, five taps and two keyboard swaps per fraction, MathLive's full keyboard in front of children, and an admin path that never promoted `1/2`. A picture can ride along (content-type checked, an ordinary `QuestionImage`), and the answer is either a value that must satisfy `ExactValue.parse` or a set of multiple-choice options (at least two, at least one marked correct). It builds an ordinary question with `status: in_review` and files a **ticket** on it (`QuestionReport` with `kind: :suggestion`, `QuestionReport.file_suggestion!`; the suggester's optional note is its first word, the snapshot is version 1, and `reason` is null — it is required of a report only, since none of the seven reasons is true of a suggestion and a fake one would count as a complaint). The team decides it in the inbox or in **review mode** (`/overseer/inbox/review`, `Overseer::ReviewsController`): the waiting suggestions one at a time, oldest first, the problem as a student sees it with its topic and Elo editable inline, the author's rating and accepted count, `SuggestionChecks` (the answer reads as a value / an option is marked, the explanation mentions every fraction in the text, fewer than three options, an identical `body_text`, the nearest published problems of the topic, and a suggested Elo — the median of published problems on the same template, `SessionComposer::SHAPE_SQL`), and four decisions plus skip on keys A/E/R/X/J (`review_keys_controller.js`, silent while typing). Accepting (`accept!`) publishes the question and closes the ticket resolved; returning (`decline!`) parks it in `private_library` and closes it dismissed, with a required note; asking is a comment, which waits on the author. The author answers in the thread or by changing the problem at `/suggestions/:id/edit` while it waits — recorded in the thread as an `edited` message, „промени задачата · версия 2“, which puts it back in front of the team. Existing suggestions were given their tickets by the migration, the status read off the question. Suggestion tickets appear wherever reports do — „Моите сигнали“, the count on „Аз“, the replies on „Днес“, the parent's „Сигнали“ tab and `report_activity` mail — worded as suggestions (`ReportsHelper`); `report_filed` is not sent for one, since a suggestion is nothing wrong. `elo` starts at the suggester's own rating (people propose problems from where they stand — a guess, which review mode corrects), and a duplicate of an existing `body_text` is refused the same way the importer dedupes. `questions.suggested_by_id` is separate from `author_id` on purpose: an authored question carries an author and no byline, but a suggestion is credited wherever it is asked — `shared/_suggested_by` renders „Предложи я: …“ (nickname first, real name only as a fallback, the leaderboard's privacy stance) in the practice-card meta row and on the duel card. The suggester follows their problems on the same page under waiting/accepted/returned, each linked to its conversation — the internal draft/private_library statuses a rejection parks a question in are not their business.
