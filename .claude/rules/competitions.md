# Competitions, exams and past papers

**Competitions and exams** are the second way in from search, and the one with a season. The calendar is data with a year in it: `competitions` (one row per competition or exam), `competition_editions` (one per year — СМТ 2026, СМТ 2027 — with that year's `format` when it differed) and `competition_rounds` (an edition's dates, each `approximate` until published), edited by the team at `/overseer/competitions` (`Overseer::CompetitionsController`, „Състезания“ in the admin menu) and read through `Competitions`, which turns them into the same `Event`/`Round` values as before plus `editions` (`current_edition`, `edition(year)`), once per request (`Current.competitions`). „Ново издание (+1 година)“ copies the last edition's rounds a year on, every date approximate; `CompetitionAttention` flags a competition with no next date, an approximate one, or a held edition whose paper is not filed though earlier ones are. A child enters an edition by its season (`competition_entries.season`), a past paper belongs to one by year, and the public page shows the current edition's rounds and format and then the past papers the bank holds (`competition_pages/_past_papers`, driven by `PastPapers.available`, not by the calendar's editions): a `.segmented` class picker (`past_papers_controller.js`, every grade rendered so search engines read them all, the student's own grade first), the newest three years as cards with size, minutes and a student's last result, the older ones as rows with the first six shown and the rest behind „Покажи още“, each opening that year's paper (`new_competition_paper_path` with `grade` and `year`). `db/seeds/competitions.yml` was the first import (`rails competitions:import`, idempotent; `db/seeds.rb` and the spec suite run it, adding an edition for every year a past paper is filed under) and is still the seed. It holds every Bulgarian maths competition worth a parent's search — the Kangaroo, the olympiad, the СМБ tournaments — plus the four exams (НВО 4/7/10 and the матура, `kind: exam`), each with its organiser, grades, marking `style` (`choice`/`answer`/`mixed`/`proof`), rounds with dates, both locales, and the bank topics a preparation session draws on (`topics:`, or `programme: [from, to]` for the union of `Curriculum::TOPICS` over those grades, which is what an exam asks). A date that has not been published is in the file as `approximate: true`, set from last year's, and **says „около“ everywhere it is shown** (`.event-date.is-approximate`, dashed) and is left out of the `Event` JSON-LD — a parent planning around a guess we presented as fact stops believing the calendar. The public half is `CompetitionPagesController`: `/sastezaniya` (month-by-month dates, then the competitions, then the exams, then what the app does *not* prepare for), `/sastezaniya/:slug` and `/izpiti/:slug` (one canonical address each — the wrong prefix 301s), each page leading with the date, then format and rounds, then worked problems from the event's topics sampled across its Elo window (the grades' `Curriculum` windows, lifted by `Competitions::SAMPLE_LIFT` for a competition), then an honest paragraph per `style` — Wunderkind checks answers, not written solutions, so a proof olympiad is told so — then an FAQ mirrored exactly into `FAQPage`. The inside half: a student says „Ще участвам“ (`CompetitionEntriesController`, one `competition_entries` row per user × slug × **season** — the school year of the event's next round, so last spring's Kangaroo entry is not this spring's; `CompetitionEntry.upcoming_for` is the read), sees the nearest entered competition counting down on the home page (`home/_competitions`, or the next three dates when they have entered none — nobody enters a competition they never heard was on), and lists them all at `/competitions`. A visitor who presses the button on a public page signs up with `?competition=slug` (both roles' forms carry it): a student's account is entered, and either role's session remembers it (`ApplicationController#signup_competition`), so the placement welcome says preparation comes next and its result leads straight into „Подготви се" for it, and a parent's first child is entered in it — for an exam, the child's practice tab opens on that exam's preset. „Подготви се" is offered only where `CompetitionPractice.ready?` (`MIN_QUESTIONS` of the event's problems near the child); otherwise the button is ordinary practice and says why, and a press that finds too few starts today's daily session with a notice rather than an error. „Подготви се“ is `CompetitionPractice`: a `kind: :competition` session (`assignments.competition_slug`) of the event's topics only, aimed `LIFT` points above the student's everyday target (≈55% rather than 70%, since competition problems sit above school ones by design; an exam gets no lift), capped at `SessionComposer::MAX_PER_SHAPE` per template, short rather than padded off-topic. It **never asks the child's grade** — the event's grades say who the paper is for, the session aims its topics at the child's rating like every other one — and it is measured, hinted and skippable like ordinary practice, because these are fresh problems chosen by `Dispatcher` (`pick(..., lift:)`). Its summary screen offers another round for the same event. An exam (not a competition) also has „Пробен изпит" (`MockExam`, `POST /competitions/:slug/mock`, `kind: :mock`, a row on „Упражнения" for an entered exam and a button beside „Подготви се"): `FORMATS` problems (20, or 15 for НВО 4) from the exam's topics spread evenly across the exam's own grade window (`Event#elo_range`) rather than the child's rating, easiest first, one per template; a time limit with a countdown in the practice header, after which the paper ends (`MockExam.over?`, checked on the question, the answer and the summary — the server is the only clock) and unanswered problems count as not done; no hints, no feedback card, no verdict or sound per answer and no coloured progress dots until the end; the summary scores it out of the whole paper and offers the weakest topics as „Тренирай тема". It says on the result that it is a practice test, not the official format or scoring — the real formats are a later step. **Past papers** („Минали теми", `PastPapers`, `PastPapersController` at `/competitions/:slug/papers`) are real papers of a competition or МОН exam, word for word: one file per paper, `db/seeds/past/<competition slug>/<grade>/<year>.yml` — the paper's minutes and instructions (older papers print neither, so they are sat in `DEFAULT_MINUTES` and scored as a share rather than in points nobody published), each task's text, options in the printed order, the official answer, a worked explanation, its points and its kind (choice / short / written), the figure cropped from the page under `db/seeds/images/past/…`, and under `skipped` every task the app cannot grade (pictures as options, several answers, a proof) with the reason. They are written by reading each page as an image (never `pdftotext`, which loses fractions and figures) and checking the answer against the official key; a key we believe wrong stays in the file with a `note` and `status: draft`. `rake past_papers:import` loads them; each question carries `questions.source` (jsonb: kind, competition, grade, year, number, points, task) and is matched on re-import by that, not by its words — two papers both ask „Кое е вярно?" over different options. The label on the problem (`shared/_question_source`, `QuestionSourcesHelper`) is „СМТ 2024 · 3 клас · задача 5" — the organiser's competition or exam, never the site a paper was found on. A competition of several rounds a year (Математика без граници: autumn, winter, spring, semi-final, final, relays) files each round as `<year>-<round>.yml`, the round one of `PastPapers::ROUNDS` (Latin slugs, named in the locales under `past_papers.rounds`, ordered by the month they are sat in), and the round is part of the paper everywhere: `source.round`, `ProblemSeeds.source_key`, `assignments.paper_round`, the `round` param on the paper links, `PastPapers.edition` („2025 · Есен“) in every title and label. A file with no round is the competition's `MAIN_ROUND` (spring for МБГ) and keeps the source it had, so the 700 spring problems already live never moved; a suffix that is not a round is refused on import. Figures of a round paper are `fig-<year>-<round>-<task>[-<level>].png`, which `past_figures.rb` gets for free from the file name. A past paper is sat as a `mock` assignment with `paper_year`/`paper_grade`: its tasks in its order, its own minutes (`MockExam.minutes_for`), no help until the end, scored in its points (`MockExam.result`), with the tasks it cannot check named on the result. `<year>-modelled.yml` beside a paper holds ours written on its model — easy, medium and hard of each type (`source.kind: modelled`, `level`), labelled „По модела на задача 5 · СМТ 2024, 3 клас" — and „Подготви се" for a competition draws half its session from that competition's own problems (`Dispatcher.pick(source:)`). A problem in those files may also carry a `figure:` — a `Figures` function and its arguments — drawn by `tools/authoring/past_figures.rb` with the corpus's own figure library and rasterized by `rasterize.sh` (`tmp/authoring/past-manifest.tsv`) to `fig-<year>-<task>[-<level>].png` beside the scans, which `PastPapers` attaches when the problem names no image; so a drawn figure in the bank speaks one figure language. `Figures.balances` (scales holding labelled weights, shapes or words, a beam tipped to the side that went down, several to a picture laid out to stay under `MAX_WIDTH`, pans stacking rows past `PAN_ROW`) and `Figures.bar_model` (bars on one scale, a difference bracket, a total) were added for them; a figure shows only what the text says — the unknown as „?“, never the answer or the step that finds it (a picture that would, such as 2022 task 2's, is left out). The first is the Софийски математически турнир, 3rd grade, 2000–2025 (318 problems). `Analytics::EVENTS` gained `competition_joined` (add the „Competition Joined“ goal in Plausible by hand). The landing page opens, after the hero, on five doors for the five reasons people arrive — the exams (with their dates), a competition (with the next three), getting better (grades and topics), thinking sharper (`StaticPagesController::THINKING_TOPICS`), and duels — because the rest of the page only ever spoke to the third.
