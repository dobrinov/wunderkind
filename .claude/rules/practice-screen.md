# The practice screen

A right or wrong answer also makes a sound — two synthesized notes each, rising for right and lower and falling for wrong, in `app/javascript/lib/sounds.js`; nothing ships as an audio file, and the cues are demoed audibly at `/design-system`. That cue is why `AnswersController#create` answers one POST two ways (`advance_to`): `format.html` redirects as it always did, `format.json` returns `{verdict, redirect}` for `answer_form_controller`'s `fetch`. The split is not decoration. Chrome hands a new document the previous one's user activation, but **WebKit and Firefox do not** — an `AudioContext` created on a freshly loaded page stays suspended with its `resume()` promise pending forever — so a chime played when the feedback page renders is silent on an iPad. The only page allowed to start audio is the one the student clicked on, so the answer is posted from it and it is left standing for the length of the cue before the next page is asked for. A skip and a `pending_review` answer carry no verdict and no sound, being neither right nor wrong. `users.sound_effects` is the switch, and it is one column in two places that therefore cannot disagree: the speaker button (`shared/_sound_toggle`, in the practice header and the duel scoreboard, saved by `SoundSettingsController` at `PATCH /profile/sound` — its own endpoint because `ProfilesController#update` would answer a mid-question press with a redirect and a „Успешно обновление!" toast) and a row in Настройки. With sound off the form submits natively exactly as before, and the fetch path never re-posts on failure — it reloads, because one answer per question is a database constraint and the POST may already have landed. Duels sound the same way, through `ChallengeAnswersController`. `users.feedback_after_answer` (default on since 2026-10-29, existing accounts left as they were) decides whether an answer shows its feedback card — verdict and worked explanation — before the next problem (`AnswersController#next_path`). The question screen (`assignment_questions/show`) lays out side by side on a landscape screen from 1024px (`.practice-layout`; `min-aspect-ratio: 1/1`, because an upright iPad reads best as one column and a sideways one has ~650px of height): once answered, the problem on the left and the feedback beside it; before answering, only a problem with a picture or a tall widget (`ApplicationHelper::TALL_WIDGETS`, `split_prompt?`) splits, so „Провери" is on screen without scrolling past the figure. „Чернова" (`shared/_scratchpad`, `scratchpad_controller.js`) is squared paper to work on with a finger, or to type numbers onto (the „Текст" tool: tap the paper, type, Enter): marks — pen and eraser strokes as points, typed notes as a position and a string — are redrawn on a canvas (so resize and undo are free, and the eraser is a `destination-out` stroke undo can bring back), kept in `sessionStorage` under the question so they survive the reload to the feedback card (and the paper reopens there if it was open), never sent anywhere. A bottom sheet on a phone or upright tablet, a 400px panel in landscape that pushes the practice column over (and drops it to one column) rather than covering the answer controls. It has two doors, the header's pencil and „Смятай на черновата" under the answer controls (`shared/_scratchpad_button`), because while it was only in the header 2 of 12 children in a simulated family test ever opened it; and on a landscape screen it opens by itself beside a problem `ApplicationHelper#scratchpad_suggested?` names (a figure, or `SCRATCHPAD_TEXT_LENGTH` characters of word problem; never a widget, which is its own working surface) until the child closes one it opened, which is remembered for the device (`localStorage` `scratchpad-auto`) until they open it by hand again. Duels carry it too, under the answer, never opened by itself — in a race it would take the room the clock is kept on screen by. `Scratchpad Opened` is sent only for a hand-opened paper; `Scratchpad Used` (the first mark, with how the paper was opened) is what the KPI reads. „Прочети ми" (`shared/_read_aloud_button`, `read_aloud_controller.js`, in the problem's meta row) reads the problem and its options aloud with the device's own Bulgarian voice (`speechSynthesis` — nothing is sent or downloaded), because a first-grader in the family field test could not use the app without a parent reading for them. The button is rendered `hidden` and shown only once a `bg` voice turns up (iOS lists voices late, hence `voiceschanged`): a voice for another language reading Cyrillic is worse than none. What it says is the page, not the stored text — the options are shuffled per visit — passed through `lib/speakable.js` (`textOf`: a `.frac` by its aria-label, a KaTeX formula by its `data-math-latex-value`; `speakable`: „3/4" → „3 върху 4", „·" → „по", „−" → „минус", ² → „на квадрат"…), whose cases are a node test run by `spec/javascript/speakable_spec.rb`. Up to 2nd grade (`SchoolGrade`, and preschool) it is a larger tinted chip. Practice only: in a duel the seconds it takes to listen are scored. `Read Aloud` is sent on the first press per problem. Explanations and answer chips set every bare fraction stacked through `math_text` (the `RichContent::FRACTION` rule), so a card no longer shows ¾ in the problem and „27/36" in its „Защо". The session summary reads its XP from `Assignment#xp_earned` (answers plus the session bonus, from `xp_events`), not the flash, which only held the last answer's share; its „Упражни ги" posts `assignment_id` to `ReviewsController#practice`, so `MistakePractice` draws only from that session's still-wrong questions (`from:`). Its score messages encourage rather than judge, because sessions are pitched near 70% on purpose. A placement's summary redirects to its result, which leads to „Днес" (where the tour runs) rather than straight into a session.
