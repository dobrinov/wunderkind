# Public pages, SEO, analytics, admin stats

The landing page (`StaticPagesController#landingpage`, the `root`) is the one screen a visitor sees. Its artwork *is* the product: a real `.practice-card` with `.choice-button`s and a real `.fb-band` inside a `.landing-peek` frame, the parent's `shared/_rating_band_card`, and duel chrome — no illustrations and no stock photography, and amber appears in exactly one place, inside the screenshot, where it still means "you earned something". Every figure on the page is read off the constant that implements it (`Challenge::SECONDS_PER_QUESTION`, `ChallengeScoring::CORRECT_POINTS`/`SPEED_POINTS`, `DailyPractice::DEFAULT_MINUTES`, `Placement::QUESTION_COUNT`, `Dispatcher::TARGET_SUCCESS_RATE`, `AnswerSubmission::DEFERRAL_DAYS`, `RatingBand::BANDS`, `Widgets::REGISTRY`) so a claim cannot drift away from the code; the only figures that are not constants are the bank's, from `BankStats` (app/services/bank_stats.rb): the published count in the proof strip and the „Банката задачи“ section (`static_pages/_bank`) — totals, answer-kind shares, widget kinds, real past-paper tasks and their years, problems with a figure, problems per grade linking to the grade pages — counts only, never a problem. The snapshot is cached under a fingerprint of the questions table (published count + newest `updated_at`, one cheap query per visit), so an import, a retirement or a deletion shows on the next visit rather than next day; only a raw `update_all` that skips `updated_at` slips past, until the week's expiry. Counts are floored (hundreds from a thousand up, tens below — `BankStats.floor`), the explained share is computed rather than claimed, and below `BankStats::MINIMUM` the section disappears and the strip shows the daily-minutes tile instead. There is no pricing, no usage metric and no testimonial, because none of the three exist yet. The `.landing-*` primitives and `.button-hero` are documented at `/design-system` under „Публична страница“.

**„Опитай без профил"** (`TrialsController`, `Trial`) lets a visitor answer `Trial::LENGTH` real problems before signing up, and every public page offers it: „Опитай“ in the header, the landing hero's second button, and the CTA bands' secondary button for visitors (`curriculum/_cta` and `competition_pages/_cta`, of the page's own deck). Each page's link names what it deals, a `Trial::Deck`, in one URL segment: `/opitay/3-klas` is a grade (`Curriculum`'s window and programme topics), `/opitay/tema-drobi` a topic (topic and explainer pages), `/opitay/<slug>` a competition or exam (its hero and CTA). A competition whose papers the bank holds first asks for the grade (`trials/choose`, only grades with `LENGTH` gradable problems, skipped when there is one). `/opitay/<slug>-3-klas` then deals that grade's own problems, originals and modelled (by `questions.source`), with the paper's source pill, and each past-papers grade panel links its own. Without papers, or with too few, a competition deals from its topics in its grades' window. `/opitay` itself picks a grade or a competition. One problem is drawn at random from each fifth of the Elo the deck's pool occupies, so the set climbs, and each from a topic the set has not had yet where its fifth has one (`Trial.unlike`, the same rule as the page samples' `Curriculum.other_topic`). The draw is random, outside the query cache, because a person uses this page and a crawler doesn't (it is `noindex`, and `robots.txt` disallows `/opitay`). The pool (`Trial.gradable`) is published problems that have an explanation and are multiple choice, exact value or a widget, except the drawn-answer widgets (`WidgetAnswersHelper::DRAWN_ANSWERS`), whose read-back needs a stored answer. **Nothing touches the database**: the deck key, ids and answers live in `session[:trial]`, `Grading.grade` judges each answer, and no Elo moves, because a visitor is not a calibrated student. The screen is the practice screen's own pieces (modal layout, `.practice-card`, `answers/_inputs`, „Чернова", „Прочети ми") with its own header and `trials/_feedback`. The feedback card always shows, and a right answer offers its solution folded. It has no sound switch, „Не съм го учил", hints or report link, because all of those belong to an account. The result page lists the five problems, pitches the account with figures read off constants, and links to sign-up (carrying `competition:` for a competition deck), „Още 5 задачи" and another grade or back to the page. A signed-in user is sent home. Analytics: `trial_started` and `trial_finished` through `track`, with `Deck#props` (kind, grade, competition) and `correct`, plus `trial_answered` from markup, and the `trial` funnel, the `trial_to_signup` KPI and a „Try without an account" feature under Public pages in `trace.json`.

Search: the app is almost entirely behind a login, so `Seo` (app/services/seo.rb) makes `noindex, nofollow` the default and a page opts in with `seo title:, description:, canonical_path:, indexable: true` (the `SeoPage` concern, read by `shared/_head`). That default is not about leaking — the authenticated pages are unreachable without a session anyway — it is that a crawler following `/calendar` gets a redirect to `/sign-in`, and a dozen copies of the sign-in page is the whole of what a site this size gets crawled for. `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are generated by `SitemapsController` rather than kept in `public/`, because both have to know which pages exist and a static file would go stale the first time a topic was added; a host that is not `Seo::DEFAULT_ORIGIN` closes itself to crawlers outright. The one public JSON-LD block is site-wide (`Seo.site_schema`), plus `FAQPage` on the landing page — mirroring the six FAQ entries that are actually rendered, from the same translation keys — and `Course`/`BreadcrumbList` on the content pages. `public/og-card.png` is the 1200×630 sharing card (Viber and Facebook groups, not Twitter); it is rasterized from `tools/og/card.html.erb` by `rake og_card:build`, an ERB template rendered inside Rails for the same reason the landing page reads its numbers off constants — the card once said „10 минути“ while `DailyPractice::DEFAULT_MINUTES` said 15, and a picture is the one surface where nobody would catch it.

Analytics: Plausible, loaded by `shared/_analytics` out of `shared/_head` (so all five layouts carry it) and switched by `Analytics.enabled?` — production only, and never for an admin, because the owner is the heaviest user of this app and on a site this size their own sessions and every `/overseer` page would be most of the dashboard. Signing out is how you check the script is live. The script is served from a first-party subdomain (`Analytics::SCRIPT_ORIGIN`, env-overridable) not because blockers are to be dodged but because a good share of parents run one and a dashboard that silently omits them is worse than none. Pageviews need no help — the script hooks `history.pushState`, which is what Turbo navigates with — but they do need `Analytics::PATH_NORMALIZER`, a `transformRequest` that folds any path segment of bare digits to `:id`: untouched, thirty thousand questions would be thirty thousand Top Pages rows of one visit each and the number that matters would not appear at all. Only bare digits, so `/matematika/3-klas` and `/matematika/tema/drobi` keep their own rows, which are the ones worth reading; the query string is kept whole, because Plausible reads utm_* off it and this product is shared by parents pasting links into Facebook groups. `Analytics::EVENTS` is the closed list of goals — a Plausible goal matches its name character for character, so the names live there and `track :session_started, kind: …` (the `TrackedEvents` concern) resolves one, raising on a typo rather than leaving a goal at zero for a month. An event rides the flash to the page the redirect lands on and is written into its `<head>` as a meta tag (provisional, so Turbo swaps it per visit where a `<script>` would pile up), read there by `lib/analytics.js` on `turbo:load`. `Duel Finished` is the one exception, sent from `challenge_controller.js` when the poll sees the status change, because that is the only moment that happens exactly once per player per match — a result screen can be reopened all week and a match can end on the clock with neither player making a request — and it goes through `trackThen`, which holds the reload until the event is away or 400ms have passed. Nothing sent carries a user id, a nickname or an email — Trace alone gets `Analytics.trace_identity`, an HMAC of the id under a key derived from secret_key_base, as a `trace-identity` meta tag that `lib/analytics.js` hands to `trace.identify` on `turbo:load`, so it can count daily and monthly actives and retention without being able to name anyone; every property is a low-cardinality enum already in the schema (role, session kind, report reason, duel result). Trace (tracetells.com, the behaviour analytics in ../schema/telltale) runs beside Plausible under the same switch: `Analytics.trace_script_url` in the same partial, cookieless, so it stores nothing on the device and shows no consent bar, and `lib/analytics.js` sends every event to both, Trace under the EVENTS key (`session_started`). Two moments go to Trace alone, from markup: `data-trace-event` on the answer form (`problem_answered`) and on the skip button (`problem_skipped`), because one per question is more than the flash's one-event slot can carry and the last answer already uses it for `session_completed`. The redesign's own moments are measured too, so its thresholds can be set from data: `tour_finished` and `scratchpad_opened` from the browser (`track` from `lib/analytics.js`), `introduction_answered`, `child_added`, `daily_minutes_set` and `features_shown_all` from their controllers, with the `new_student` (sign-up → placement → tour → two finished sessions) and `new_parent` (sign-up → child added → child's page → switch-in → first session) funnels and KPIs for tour completion, parent activation, focus adoption, introduction uptake and scratch-paper use. Its KPIs and event catalogue are `trace.json` at the repository root (`trace push`, or push_config over MCP, after editing); `data-trace-mask` sits on every view that shows a name, and /overseer is ignored by a rule on the Trace side.

**Admin statistics** (`/overseer/stats`, `Overseer::StatsController`, `AdminStats`) are the database's view, where Plausible and Trace are the visits'. Scope is everybody, one family (`family=<parent id>`, the parent's children) or one user (`user_id=`), picked from a plain `<select>` of recently seen accounts that posts `who=f12`/`u5` and is redirected to those params; ranges are `AdminStats::RANGES` (7/30/90/365 — the student pages keep `AnswerStats::RANGES`). Every headline is set against the same number of days before it: active students per week (distinct students with an *attempted* answer per seven-day window back from today, averaged over the range's whole weeks), problems, minutes, the correct share against `Dispatcher::TARGET_SUCCESS_RATE` (uncoloured — it is aimed at, not maximised) and the skip share (falling is green). The daily chart is `shared/_answer_stats` over a set of users (`AnswerStats.new(user_ids:)`, `totals: false`); a day still opens only for one user. Under everybody: nested funnels for students and parents who signed up in the range (placed → first finished non-placement session → two; child added → a managed child's `last_login_at`, which the switch-in stamps → that child finished a session), and retention for the last six Monday sign-up cohorts, week *n* being days 7n…7n+6 after each student's own sign-up and filled only once over for the whole cohort. Above the funnels, sign-ups and sign-ins by day (`AdminStats#account_days`, `overseer/stats/_accounts`): sign-ups off `users.created_at`, students and parents stacked and the child profiles a parent added counted apart, since nobody signed up; sign-ins off `sign_ins`, one row per `User#logged_in!(via:)` (`SignIn::VIAS` — password, signup, email_verification, switch), a switch into a managed child's profile stacked on the rest and the session a sign-up starts with not counted again. `users.last_login_at` only ever held the latest, so sign-ins have no history before that table and nothing was backfilled — a chart of last sign-ins would draw the past quieter than it was; days before `sign_ins_since` are blank and say so. Sessions never expire, so sign-ins are how often people have to get back in, not who came. Then skip share by topic (`MIN_TOPIC_ANSWERS`), the bank (distinct questions answered or skipped in the range against the published count; new rows and non-admin suggestions with how many are published now) and duels (finished, by invitation, rooms that never started, median queue wait from `created_at` to `paired_at`). Two things are deliberately missing because no column dates them: when a question was retired and when a suggestion was accepted — and an expired room and one backed out of are both `abandoned`, so they are counted together. Admin accounts are out of every number.

The public content pages (`CurriculumController`: `/matematika`, `/matematika/:n-klas`, `/matematika/tema/:slug`) are the part that can actually rank. The landing page can only ever rank for the brand, and a parent who has not heard of Wunderkind types „задачи по математика за 3 клас" instead — so these pages carry a real slice of the bank with the worked explanation under each problem, the answer on the page rather than behind a sign-up („с отговори" is the commonest thing a query adds), and links onward to every grade and topic. Samples are deterministic — a page that shows different problems on every fetch teaches a crawler not to cache it — and taken at evenly spaced points across the topic's *occupied* Elo window rather than off the front of it, since the first six of a six-thousand-problem range are the same problem in different numbers. Only multiple-choice and exact-value questions are shown: an interactive question's answer is a widget state with no reading as a line of text. A multiple-choice sample's options are real buttons (`sample_choice_controller`): a tap marks the pick right or wrong, shows the right one, and reveals „Още задачи като тази" (sign-up, carrying `competition:` on a competition page) — Trace found visitors tapping them when they were inert `<li>`s styled as `.choice-button`. The landing page's pictured answers are `pointer-events-none` for the same reason: they are a picture, not a control. `Topic#slug` is now a transliteration of the Bulgarian name through `Slug` (the official streamlined table, as in passports) — it used to be `SecureRandom.hex`, because `parameterize` folds Cyrillic to the empty string, which was harmless while nothing linked to a topic and useless the moment one became a URL. A slug follows the name: renaming a topic moves its page, and the address it leaves goes into `topics.previous_slugs`, which `CurriculumController#topic` answers with a 301 (no slug once handed out is given to another topic). The admin edits the tree at `/overseer/topics` (`Overseer::TopicsController`): a tree / by-grade / prerequisite-map index read off `TopicStats`' grouped queries with a `TopicDetail` side panel in a turbo-frame, and an editor that keeps the tree two levels deep (validations in the `:editor` context only — an area with topics stays an area, a topic with questions stays a topic, only an area can be a parent) and refuses a prerequisite that would close a loop (`TopicPrerequisite.blocked_for`, also a model validation). A topic with questions is never deleted, only merged (`TopicMerge`): questions, prerequisites both ways (self-loops and duplicates dropped, a merge that would close a loop refused), focus topics and their starting ratings and duel categories move; a student with a skill in both keeps the row with more games (a tie keeps the target's) rather than any blend of two calibrations; the merged slug joins the target's `previous_slugs`. Names still matter to code: `Curriculum::TOPICS`, the explainers, `competitions.yml` and `topics.yml` find topics by name, so a rename there needs a code change too — the editor says so.

**„Обяснено просто"** (`ExplainersController`, `/matematika/obyasneno` and `/matematika/obyasneno/:slug`) answers the other half of what a parent searches: not „задачи за прости числа" but „какво е просто число". Forty-three articles, one for every topic of `Curriculum::TOPICS` (perimeter and area share one; vectors has no topic of its own) — each a short plain explanation with a playground to try the idea in, the common traps, a self-check whose answers sit in `<details>` (in the page for a crawler and a screen reader, one tap away for everyone else), and the topic's worked problems from the bank via `curriculum/_samples`. The text is in `config/explainers/<slug>.yml`, one file per article with both locales, read by `Explainers` the way `Competitions` reads its file — text, not templates, because an article is edited by someone fixing a sentence. Paragraphs are plain text with `**bold**`, escaped before `explainer_text` puts the emphasis back, so a file can carry no markup. A playground is a partial in `app/views/explainers/playgrounds/` plus a Stimulus controller in `app/javascript/controllers/explainers/`, registered lazily (each is used on one public page), its strings in the article's `playgrounds:` copy rather than in the JS, and its card the shared `.explainer-play`/`.play-*` classes. `spec/requests/explainers_spec.rb` refuses an article missing a locale, a playground, a partial, a registered controller, a real topic or a related article that exists. It also holds every sentence to being a string: one written without quotes and holding „ : " (a division, a ratio) is read by YAML as a key and a value, and six such were printed as hashes on live pages — quote any value with `: ` in it. `e2e/specs/explainers.spec.mjs` loads every article at desktop and phone width (the controller connected, no console error, no sideways scroll, 44px controls, figure text at least 10.5px on a phone — four of the first eight are exempt by name until redrawn) and tests some exercises' arithmetic. A playground's strings all come from the article's `playgrounds:` copy; an action button is `.play-key.is-primary`, and `aria-pressed` is only for a key that is a choice among keys; figure text is sized so it stays readable when the SVG shrinks to a phone (a 360-wide viewBox with 14-unit text, not 520 with 13). The grades are the МОН programme's, like the grade pages; the topic pages link their article, and both hub and articles are in the sitemap with `LearningResource` and breadcrumb JSON-LD.

`Curriculum` (app/services/curriculum.rb) is the grade ladder, and it is worth being blunt about why it exists at all, given that **there are no school grades anywhere in the model** and nothing above changes that: it is read by no part of the answer flow, stored on no user and no question, and lives entirely in the presentation layer. But every parent in the country searches in grades, and a site that refuses to say „клас" is a site they never find — so the public pages speak grades at the door and the app behind the door still speaks Elo. The grade→Elo windows are editorial and deliberately overlap, which is the same thing the app says once a child is inside; a grade page lists a topic only when `Curriculum::TOPICS` (the МОН programme's topics for that grade, mapped by hand) names it *and* the window holds `MINIMUM_PER_TOPIC` problems of it — the window alone put logarithms on the seventh-grade page, because Elo says how hard a problem is and not when school teaches it. The ladder runs 1st to 12th grade, its windows widening and overlapping more from 8th grade on (a routine logarithm rates below a hard quadratic, so above 7th grade it is the topic list doing the work). `/matematika/4-klas`, `/7-klas` and `/10-klas` carry an НВО section and `/12-klas` a ДЗИ (матура) one, each putting its exam in the title (`Curriculum::EXAMS`), because those are queries with their own season.

**The hub and a grade's pages.** `/matematika` (`curriculum/index`) is a router: a grade picker whose twelve panels are all in the HTML (each tile is a plain link to the grade page; `grade_picker_controller` only shows one panel instead of leaving), each panel listing that grade's problems, test, formula sheet, exam page and topics; then the three everyday pages (table, formulas, articles) and the topic directory with a search (`list_filter_controller`, shared with the formula sheets: it hides items by `data-text`, then empty groups). A grade's pages are siblings under one strip (`curriculum/_grade_nav`): the grade page, `/matematika/:n-klas/test` and its formula sheet, plus the exam page on 4/7/10/12. **`/matematika/:n-klas/:slug`** (`CurriculumController#grade_topic`) is one topic in one grade — „текстови задачи за 2 клас" is its own search, answered by neither the grade page nor the topic page. It exists only where the grade page would list the topic (`Curriculum.grade_topic?`: the programme teaches it there and the window holds `MINIMUM_PER_TOPIC`), follows `previous_slugs` with a 301, and carries the topic's formulas for that grade; the grade page's topic links and the topic page's grade links point at it. **The test** (`GradeTestsController`, `Curriculum.test_questions`: `TEST_LENGTH` problems spread over the window and none of them the grade page's samples) is a paper, not a session: one form, radio buttons drawn as `.choice-button`s and plain text fields, POSTed with `data-turbo="false"` and re-rendered marked by the real `Grading` — so „24,0" is 24 as in the app — with the verdict, the answer and the solution under each problem. Nothing is stored, no Elo moves, and answers to problems not on the paper are ignored.

**Formulas** (`Formulas`, `config/formulas.yml`, `FormulasController`): every formula and rule of grades 1–12, filed under a leaf topic with the grade it is first taught in (`from`), in both locales, as plain Unicode rather than LaTeX so a sheet prints and is found by a search for „a² + b²". `/matematika/formuli` is all of them; `/matematika/formuli/:view` is one grade (`7-klas`) or one exam sheet (`Formulas::SHEETS`: НВО 4 = grades 1–4, НВО 7 = 5–7, НВО 10 = 8–10, `matura` = 8–12) — separate pages because „формули 7 клас" and „формули нво 10 клас" are separate searches. A view shows the formulas of the topics its grades teach (`Curriculum::TOPICS`) with `from` at or below its last grade. The example under a formula is a `<details>`; the search only hides cards. `spec/services/formulas_spec.rb` refuses a topic that is not a leaf, a `from` outside the grades that teach the topic, a missing locale, and any topic without a formula other than „Текстови задачи" and „Логически задачи". `Formulas::CALCULATORS` names the thirteen figure/solid calculator pages a formula may point at (`calculator:`); a card links its calculator once that page exists.

**Calculator pages** (`/matematika/formuli/:slug`, `CalculatorsController`, `Calculators`): one figure or solid each, because „лице на триъгълник" is the name of a figure and neither a formula card nor an article about area is its page. A page is a file in `config/calculators/<slug>.yml` (both locales; each mode's sliders with their ranges and starting values, its formula and a worked example as text, so the page reads whole with no script) plus a module in `app/javascript/lib/calculators/<name>.js` registered by slug in `lib/calculators/index.js`: one function per mode that takes the sliders' values and returns the working as lines, the figure as SVG (pieces from `lib/calculators/figure.js`) and any readout that is not just a number. `calculator_controller` is the same for every page. Under the calculator: why the formula holds, the mistakes, the formula cards that name the page, and the bank's problems whose text names the figure (`match:`, at even steps of Elo; the topic's samples when fewer than four do). `spec/requests/calculators_spec.rb` refuses a file whose slug no formula names, a missing locale or input label, a slider that starts outside its range, and a slug with no registered module. All thirteen are built: six areas (`triangle.js`, `plane.js`) and seven solids (`solids.js`, drawn in an oblique projection with hidden edges dashed). `spec/javascript/calculators.test.mjs` (run by `calculators_spec.rb` there, over every file) holds each page's printed example to what its script computes for the starting numbers, in both languages, and walks every slider to both ends for a figure with no NaN in it.

**The multiplication table** (`/matematika/tablitsa-za-umnozhenie`, `TimesTablesController`): a real `<table>` plus every row written out as text, because that is what prints and what „таблицата на 7" has to find; `times_table_controller` lights a tapped cell's row and column and writes its two divisions, and „Скрий отговорите" turns cells into „?"; `times_drill_controller` asks ten questions of one row (each factor once) or of the whole table. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**Worksheets** (`WorksheetsController`: `/matematika/:n-klas/raboten-list` and `/matematika/:n-klas/:slug/raboten-list`) are paper, for „задачи … за принтиране": `Curriculum.worksheet_questions` sets `WORKSHEET_LENGTH` problems of the grade (or of one topic in it) that the grade page, the topic's page and the test do not show, and the page is two `.worksheet` sheets — the problems with a line to answer on (a multiple-choice problem's options lettered, since paper cannot be tapped), then the key on a page of its own (`break-before-page`), never the solutions. Everything else on the page is `print:hidden`. It exists only with `WORKSHEET_MINIMUM` problems, and for a topic only where the topic has a page in that grade; `Curriculum.worksheet?` gates the links (the grade strip, the grade-topic page) and the sitemap. The sheets carry no page numbers: twelve problems run to two sides and the count would be wrong.

Public pages print: the landing header and footer, breadcrumbs, pitches and controls are `print:hidden`, and `print_controller` is the „Принтирай" button.

**„Наръчник"** (`/handbook`, `HandbookController`) is the same material inside the app, in the app layout, reached from a row on „Упражнения": the formula sheet and the articles of one grade, the table for grades 1–4. It opens on `User#estimated_grade` when a birth year gives one and otherwise asks; any grade can be chosen (`?grade=`), which changes what is shown and never what is asked. It renders the public pages' own partials with `in_app: true` (`formulas/_sheet`, `explainers/_article`, `times_tables/_table` and `_practice`), which drop the landing bands and the links out; an article ends with a session on its topic (`topic_practice_path`). Not indexable, and `/handbook` is in `robots.txt`.
